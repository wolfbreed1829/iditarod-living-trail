A note about this preview

This is an independent proposal concept, not the Alliance's official website. Gifts, memberships, orders, and form submissions are demonstrations only. No payments or personal details are collected. Text, identity treatments, and products are proposed design content for review.

The final website would include the agreed content management system, approved content migration, real integrations, staff training, and accessibility verification. This preview does not claim to complete those production services.
