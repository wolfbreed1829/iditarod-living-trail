A place to begin.

At the southern end of the historic route, Seward offers an approachable connection to the trail. Start with the waterfront, discover local history, and explore the relationship between a coastal community and the journeys that began here.

Before you go

Plan for the season, respect local communities and private lands, and use current guidance from the relevant land manager. Access and conditions can change.
