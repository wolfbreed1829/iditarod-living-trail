Editorial sources

Concept copy is informed by the Alliance's existing website, the Bureau of Land Management's trail overview, and linked education and visitor resources. Historical and cultural editorial content would be reviewed with the Alliance and relevant communities before production publication.

Design & type

The trail emblem, product concepts, and route illustration are original design elements for this proposal. The route illustration is not a geographic or navigation map. DM Sans and Fraunces are distributed under the SIL Open Font License; license files accompany this preview.
