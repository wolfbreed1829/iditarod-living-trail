THE IDITAROD HISTORIC TRAIL ALLIANCE

Connected by a trail. United by its future.

The Alliance brings people together to promote, preserve, and protect the Iditarod National Historic Trail.

A SHARED RESPONSIBILITY

The trail's future
is a collective story.

The Iditarod Historic Trail Alliance is a statewide nonprofit organization founded in 1999. Its work connects education, history, recreation, and stewardship.

Through partnerships with communities, landowners, agencies, and volunteers, the Alliance helps keep the trail's heritage present and its future possible.

This design preview brings that work into focus. Organizational details and final editorial content would be developed with the Alliance.
